Chapter 35: The Envoy from Sallowmere

Envoy Petra Hollis arrived at Aldenreach Keep with the brisk efficiency of
someone who had crossed three borders in a fortnight and intended to waste no
further time on ceremony than protocol strictly demanded. Sallowmere had sent
her to finalize the Sallow Accord — a trade agreement two years in
negotiation, concerning salt rights along the shared coast — and she arrived
entirely unaware that the court receiving her was, beneath its careful
pageantry, holding together a rather larger set of secrets than any trade
agreement usually walked into.

"Lord Regent," she said, greeting Cassian with the correct degree of formal
warmth, "Sallowmere is pleased the mourning period hasn't delayed our
business unduly."

"Thornmere values the Accord as much as Sallowmere does, Envoy Hollis," Cassian
said, and if there was anything strained beneath his composure, Petra Hollis —
new to this court, unfamiliar with its particular rhythms — had no way of
detecting it.

She was shown to guest quarters, briefed on the signing's schedule by Bellamy
Thorne, and given, by Ione Ashcombe personally, a tour of the parts of the
Keep a foreign envoy was permitted to see. None of it registered, to Petra, as
anything other than the ordinary machinery of a court doing its ordinary
business.

Sorrel, passing through the outer hall with a stack of Accord-related
paperwork for the Chancery's records, caught only a glimpse of the envoy — a
composed, watchful woman in Sallowmere's blue-grey colors — and thought
nothing further of her at all. It would be several days yet before either of
them had reason to learn the other's name.
