"The Ninth House" — sample novel for StoryWeave
================================================

This original work of fiction was written specifically as test/demo data for
StoryWeave (the integration phase, DESIGN_SPEC D8 — a larger demo work to exercise
cast overload, arcs, and the chapter picker). It is released into the public domain
under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

To the extent possible under law, the author has waived all copyright and related or
neighboring rights to this work. You may copy, modify, distribute, and use it, even
for commercial purposes, all without asking permission.

It is deliberately identity-rich (aliases, a secret identity, a reincarnation, a
transmigration, and a deepening reveal across the same pair) so that GLiNER
extraction, Tier-1 structural relations, and the hand-curated Tier-2/3 identity layer
all have real, staggered material to exercise across 40 chapters. See
`docs/demo/the-ninth-house-bible.md` for the full cast, faction, arc and reveal
schedule this text was written against. Real or copyrighted novels must NOT be
committed; they belong in the gitignored `data/raw/` directory.
