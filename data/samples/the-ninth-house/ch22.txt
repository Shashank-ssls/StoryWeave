Chapter 22: A Quiet Shield

Ione Ashcombe visited the Chancery more often than her brother's business
strictly required, a habit the household attributed to her general fondness
for order and record-keeping, which was true as far as it went. She had taken
a particular interest, these past months, in the water-damaged ledger and the
scribe assigned to it — not from suspicion, but from something closer to
recognition. Ione knew what it looked like to carry more weight than anyone
around you understood, and Sorrel Quill wore that particular weight in a way
Ione found she could not simply walk past.

"Kell's been asking Vane about your attendance," Ione said, finding Sorrel
alone at her desk one evening. It was not a question.

Sorrel's hands stilled over her work. "Has he."

"He hasn't asked me. He wouldn't think to — I'm the Regent's sister, not his
watchdog." Ione's mouth curved, not quite a smile. "But I hear things, in this
Keep, whether I go looking for them or not. If you're managing something
delicate, Sorrel, I'd rather you trusted me with a little of it than have
Robart Kell decide on his own what it means."

"It's not — I can't explain it yet. Not honestly."

"Then don't." Ione set a hand briefly on the desk, not quite a touch. "I'll
tell him you've been assisting with a private matter of mine. It isn't even
untrue, if you'd let it become true. Come find me, when you're ready to
explain the rest."

She left before Sorrel could answer, which struck Sorrel, once the surprise
had faded, as its own small kindness — an offer made without demanding
gratitude for it on the spot.
