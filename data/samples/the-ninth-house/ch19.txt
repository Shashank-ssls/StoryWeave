Chapter 19: An Old Hand

Back at her own desk, ledger's final page finally unfolded by the last of
Vesper's cipher-work, Sorrel found herself looking at a list of names in a
hand she recognized — not because she knew the writer, but because she had
spent two years cataloguing Chancery archives old enough to carry the same
formal, slightly archaic loops in every letter *a*. This was not a modern
hand, copying an old style for effect. This was an old hand, full stop,
somehow appearing on a page that could not be more than a few years old by
the ledger's own binding.

She brought the observation to Vesper, who brought it, in turn, to the
Undercroft, and returned with a name Sorrel had not heard before: Kaelen's
Grimoire.

"A book," Vesper said carefully, "that the Choir has been trying to locate
for longer than either of us has been alive. If someone copied from it
recently, in a hand that old — someone has already found what we haven't."

"Copied what?"

"Names. Old Ninth House names, some of them family lines the crown recorded
as extinct generations ago." Vesper hesitated, which was unlike her. "Yours
might be one of them, Sorrel. I don't know that for certain. But I'd rather
you heard the possibility from me than stumbled into it alone."

Sorrel sat with that for a long moment, turning the water-stained ledger over
in her hands as though its binding might suddenly explain itself.

"Then I suppose," she said finally, "I'll need to see the rest of the names
myself."
