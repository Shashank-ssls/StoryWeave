Chapter 36: The Sallow Accord

The Sallow Accord was signed in the Ashen Court under a clear morning sky, a
ceremony of considerably less drama than the negotiations behind it had
involved, and considerably more drama than anyone present other than Cassian
and the small circle who knew his secret would have guessed lay beneath its
calm surface. Envoy Petra Hollis signed for Sallowmere. Cassian signed for
Thornmere, his hand steady, his composure by now well-practiced against the
private weight he carried.

Meraude Vell attended, as Council protocol required, and used the gathering
to quietly press House Vell's interests with two Sallowmere trade delegates —
the Sallow Concord's representatives, brought along to observe the signing —
without ever once appearing to press anything at all.

Among the gathered dignitaries stood Magistrate Wyle, summoned from the
southern circuit courts specifically for this season's business: with the
Choosing now only weeks away, the Council had agreed a neutral arbiter was
needed, someone with no house loyalty and a reputation for scrupulous
fairness, to oversee the ceremony and certify its outcome beyond any house's
ability to dispute it later.

"An honor, Lord Regent," Wyle said, greeting Cassian after the signing with
the measured courtesy of a man who had adjudicated enough court disputes to
trust no one's composure at face value. "I look forward to a Choosing free of
complication."

"As do I, Magistrate," Cassian said, and meant it more fervently than
propriety allowed him to show, aware — as Wyle, for now, was not — of exactly
how much complication the coming weeks were likely to hold.
